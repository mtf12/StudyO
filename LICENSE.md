## Privacy

StudyO Player collects no data, has no analytics, and no account of its own. It contacts the
internet only when you ask the AI you connected a question; that request goes straight to that
AI and contains your question, the current video frame, the file name and nearby subtitle
lines (each can be switched off in Settings). Chats are saved only on your Mac.
